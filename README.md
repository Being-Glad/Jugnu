<h1><a id="features"></a>Features</h1>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Audio & Playback Performance
- **Low-Latency Stream Pipeline**: Lightning-fast song startup & zero buffering delay
- **Open-Source AI Recommendation Engine**: Built-in intelligent recommendation engine (`RecommendationEngine.kt` & `LocalKotlinRecommender.kt`) for personalized music discovery
- Stream any song or video from YouTube Music
- Background playback & offline downloads
- Skip silence & sleep timer

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Audio Tuning & Equalizer
- **10-Band Parametric Equalizer**: 4,000+ headphone calibration presets
- Audio loudness normalization
- Tempo & pitch control
- Seamless crossfade

</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Lyrics & AI Intelligence
- **Live Synced Lyrics**: Apple-style word-by-word synchronized lyrics
- **Real-Time AI Translation**: Live AI lyrics translation with transliteration (Hindi, Furigana, Pinyin, Hangul)
- Search songs, albums, artists, videos, and playlists

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Library & Account
- Full library management
- Local & online playlists
- Import & export playlists
- YouTube Music account login & sync

</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Social
- **Listen Together Rooms**: Real-time room playback synchronization with friends

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Interface & Design
- Light, Dark, Pure OLED Pitch Black modes
- Dynamic color extraction + 19 custom accent palettes
- Built with Material 3

</td>
  </tr>
</table>
