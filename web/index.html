Features

Everything You Need for Endless Music

Packed with high-performance audio tools, live lyrics, library sync, and social features.

High Quality Streaming & Cache

Stream any track from YouTube Music. Download and cache songs for offline playback, skip silence automatically, and set sleep timers.

Live Synced Lyrics & AI Translation

Sing along with synchronized live lyrics. Translate lyrics into your language seamlessly using integrated AI translation tools.

Listen Together in Real-Time

Host or join listening rooms with friends. Play, pause, and sync your music queue live across devices in real time.

Audio Tuning & Equalizer

Take full control of your sound with custom equalizer controls, audio normalization, silence skipping, and tempo/pitch adjustments.

Material 3 & Custom Themes

Choose from Light, Dark, Pure Pitch Black (OLED), or 19 preset accent palettes plus dynamic wallpaper color extraction.

YouTube Music Account Sync

Optionally sign in to sync your YouTube Music playlists, liked songs, history, and top recommendations directly in the app.
